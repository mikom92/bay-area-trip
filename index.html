Route, day by day

Seven nights in one South Bay base — San Francisco, wine country and the coast all reached as day trips, so there is only one packing day in the whole first week.

Sun · 13 SepArrival

Sunnyvale + Palo Alto

Land at SFO 13:50 on LO35. Pick up the car and drive 45 minutes south. Staying in one place all week means no repacking until the transfer. Light first evening: a walk through Stanford, dinner in Palo Alto.

SFO Stanford University
Mon · 14 SepPilgrimage

The Silicon Valley Circuit

Computer History Museum, Apple Park and the Googleplex are all within a short drive of each other — the easiest day of the week. Limón in Mountain View does Peruvian rotisserie chicken and ceviche, right by the museum.

Computer History Museum Apple Park Googleplex Limón
Tue · 15 SepCoast

Santa Cruz

Boardwalk and Natural Bridges, 40 minutes down the coast. Worth checking whether a large conference is running in San Francisco this week — Dreamforce fills the city every September — and saving the city for a quieter day if so.

Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk Natural Bridges
Wed · 16 SepWine Country

Napa & Sonoma

Go on a weekday — thinner crowds, calmer tasting rooms. Longest drive of the easy days from the South Bay (~3–3.5h total), so build the day around it. Taste and spit; someone has to drive back.

Napa Valley Sonoma Valley
Thu · 17 SepDrive Day

Monterey & Big Sur

Highway 1 south to Bixby Bridge and the cliffs — the big drive of the week, done as an out-and-back from the South Bay.

Monterey Bixby Creek Bridge
Fri · 18 SepCity

Alcatraz + North Beach

Drive up (~45 min) and spend the afternoon in North Beach — Coit Tower, then Pier 39 for the sea lions. Dinner at Frascati around 17:00–17:30 (a few minutes from Pier 33). Night Tour check-in at 18:30 — book the earliest sailing, since the return ferry is fixed at ~21:25 regardless of which sailing you take, so the earlier check-in means more time on the island. Back in Sunnyvale by ~22:15.

Book the Alcatraz Night Tour now — 90-day window is open ↗

Sat · 19 SepCoast

Muir Woods + Point Reyes

Redwoods in the morning, cliffs and the lighthouse in the afternoon — the whole northern coast in one day. You cross the Golden Gate anyway, so stop in Sausalito for lunch and swing by Battery East Trail on the city side for one of the best bridge views around, no detour required.

Muir Woods needs a booked parking or shuttle slot ↗

What shapes this route

One base, zero relocations Every day of the first week is a round trip from the same South Bay hotel — the city, wine country and the coast all work as day trips. You unpack once. The trade-off is real though: three of those seven days run 2.5–3.5h of driving each way, and never booking a San Francisco night means never paying San Francisco rates.
Book these first Alcatraz (90-day window) and Muir Woods parking both sell out well ahead. Everything else on this route can be decided the night before.
September in the Bay Warm and dry inland, genuinely cold and windy on the coast and in the city. Pack a layer for Point Reyes and Alcatraz even when the South Bay is in the high twenties.
Second stay, mid-trip A short move up the Peninsula on day 8 — nothing to pack again until the flight home. 🔒 Sign in for the booking details.
Designated driver, wine country edition California's legal limit is 0.08% BAC — but a DUI can still apply below that if you are visibly impaired, so there is no real "safe number" while driving. Spit buckets are standard at every Napa and Sonoma tasting room; use them and let everyone else enjoy the full pour.
